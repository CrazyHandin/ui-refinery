# Sources and adaptation

UI Refinery is a renamed and modified version of [houxin0010/distinctive-ai-design](https://github.com/houxin0010/distinctive-ai-design). The modifications include task-scoped activation, workflow selection, reusable visual conventions, interaction-state guidance, and additional on-demand references. The original project and historical validation records remain attributed to their sources.

The original workflow credits Anshu Chimala's [How to turn your AI into a world-class designer](https://www.lennysnewsletter.com/p/how-to-turn-your-ai-into-a-world). This repository contains operational guidance rather than the article text. Historical notes describe which publicly accessible material was read by the upstream author.

Selected motion, data-boundary and mobile-web guidance is adapted from [emilkowalski/skills](https://github.com/emilkowalski/skills/tree/a81dbca07f2217b7103e510c0f13f8d5b017afc9), pinned to commit `a81dbca07f2217b7103e510c0f13f8d5b017afc9`.
